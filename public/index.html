How to play
  • Create a room and send the Room ID to a friend. They type it in and press Join Room.
  • Play normal chess. After every 5 turns (both players moving once = 1 turn) the board freezes and each player picks 1 of 3 cards.
  • You have 15 seconds — if time runs out a card is picked for you at random.
  • Cards come in Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Event rarity.
